How to Create Your QR Code:

  1. Enter the name, URL, and any custom text you want to include.
  2. Upload your logo if you have one. This will be embedded in the center of the QR code.
  3. Choose your preferred border color for the QR code from the available options.
  4. Click the "Generate" button to create your QR code.
  5. Once generated, click the "Download" button to save your QR code as a JPG file.

Types of QR Codes You Can Generate:

Our application allows you to create different types of QR codes based on your needs:

  • Website URL: Direct users to your event's website or any other URL.
  • Contact Information: Share your contact details, including phone number and email address.
  • Event Details: Provide detailed information about your event, such as date, time, and location.
  • Custom Text: Encode any custom message or information you want to share.
  • Wi-Fi Access: Allow users to connect to your Wi-Fi network by scanning the QR code.
  • Social Media Links: Link to your social media profiles to boost engagement.
